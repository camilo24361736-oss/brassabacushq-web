ChatShelf: folders, search and export for your AI chats

A Chrome extension that keeps your ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini conversations in one tidy side panel.

  • Folders for your chats from all three AIs, with colors, pins, notes and drag and drop
  • Search titles, folders and notes instantly; Pro also searches inside the text of your saved chats
  • Export the chat you are reading to Markdown or text (free), or PDF, HTML and JSON (Pro), with code blocks, tables, lists and math kept
  • Export a whole folder as a .zip (Pro)
  • Backups: save your folders to a file and restore them on another computer
  • English and Spanish

How to use it

  1. Install ChatShelf and pin it to the toolbar (puzzle icon → pin).
  2. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and click the ChatShelf icon. The panel opens at the side and lists your chats.
  3. Click New folder, then drag chats into it, or pick a folder for the chat you are reading at the top of the panel.
  4. To export, open the chat and click Markdown, Text, PDF, HTML or JSON. Long chats are read from the first message to the last; you can cancel at any time.
  5. To restore on a new computer: Settings (⚙) → Save backup, then Restore backup on the other computer.

Questions

Does ChatShelf delete or change my chats? No. It only reads the page. Removing a chat or folder in ChatShelf never touches your ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini account.

Where are my folders stored? Only in Chrome on your computer. Use Save backup to move them to another computer.

A site changed and export looks wrong. ChatShelf checks for updated reading rules automatically. Email us and we usually fix it the same day.
