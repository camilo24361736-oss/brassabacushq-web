StoreLens: Shopify Spy & Product Exporter

A Chrome extension for dropshippers and e-commerce sellers. Open any Shopify store, click StoreLens and see:

  • Best-selling products, in the store's own best-seller order
  • Newest products and when the last one was added
  • Theme name and version, currency and country
  • Apps and marketing pixels the store uses (reviews, email, upsell, subscriptions, chat…)
  • Catalog size, average price and price range

Export the catalog or the best sellers to a CSV in Shopify's product import format (variants, prices, images, tags) with one click.
