AdTrove: find winning ads in the Meta Ad Library

A Chrome extension for e-commerce sellers, dropshippers and marketers who research Facebook and Instagram ads in the public Meta Ad Library.

  • Days running on every ad, right on the page. Ads that run for 30+ days are usually profitable.
  • Versions: how many ads use the same creative, a sign the advertiser is scaling it.
  • Side panel to sort by days running, versions or page size, and filter by format, platform, text and store
  • Download the video or images of an ad in one click
  • Save the best ads to your own swipe file, with notes and collections (Pro)
  • Pro: bulk download, CSV export, see the store behind each ad (Shopify, WooCommerce, Tiendanube, VTEX…), hide the ads that don't match your filters, load up to 2,000 ads per search
  • English and Spanish

How to use it

  1. Install AdTrove and pin it to the toolbar (puzzle icon → pin).
  2. Open the Meta Ad Library and search for a product, keyword or competitor.
  3. Each ad shows an AdTrove bar with its days running and versions. Click ⬇ to download it or ☆ to save it.
  4. Click the AdTrove icon to open the side panel: sort, filter, load more results, and export.

Questions

Do I need a Facebook account? No account is needed by AdTrove. The Meta Ad Library is public; AdTrove works on it as you browse.

Does AdTrove send requests to Facebook on its own? No. It only reads the results the Ad Library page already loads for you. "Load more" simply scrolls the page for you.

A download failed. Video links from the Ad Library expire after a while. Reload the Ad Library page and try again.

Is AdTrove made by Meta? No. AdTrove is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.
